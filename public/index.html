What is Nuclear Energy

Nuclear energy is the energy released during nuclear fission or fusion. It is used to generate electricity in nuclear power plants around the world.

Key Facts

  • One kilogram of uranium can produce energy equivalent to three thousand tons of coal.
  • There are more than four hundred nuclear reactors operating worldwide.
  • Nuclear power plants produce no greenhouse gases during operation.
  • In 1990 nuclear power accounted for around seventeen percent of global electricity generation.

Types of Nuclear Reactions

Fission: Splitting heavy atoms to release energy.

Fusion: Combining light atoms to release energy.
